Three ways to lay out Home

Each concept is a standalone page using the real background, fonts, fire icons, 3D lit character and 3D-lip PLAY button. Numbers and profile names are sample data. Click a card to open it full size.

Concept A

Stage

Closest to today. Big wordmark and PLAY on the left, character on the right, plus a "jump back in" dock of your recent profiles.

  • Switch profile in one click without leaving Home
  • Lowest risk, easiest to build

Dashboard

Concept C

Cinematic

The photo and the character are the page. A profile pill sits at the top and one launch bar at the bottom holds PLAY and the key stats.

What stays the same in all three: the left rail with fire icons, the account chip top right, the sunset-lit character with a cast shadow, and the soft 3D-lip PLAY button. What changes: where the profile lives, how much information sits on Home, and how much of the photo is left clear.
