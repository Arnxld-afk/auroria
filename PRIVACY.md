# Auroria privacy policy

Auroria has no accounts, analytics or telemetry of its own. It does not send anything about you to the Auroria project. Everything it stores stays on your computer in `%APPDATA%\Auroria`.

Auroria talks to these services, only to do what you ask it to:

| Service | When | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| Microsoft, Xbox Live and Minecraft services | When you sign in, play, or change your skin or cape | Your sign-in, so you can play with your own Minecraft account. Sign-in tokens are stored only on your computer. |
| Mojang (piston-meta, libraries, resources) | When a version, library, asset or Java runtime needs downloading | Ordinary download requests. |
| Fabric, Quilt, Forge and NeoForge servers | When a profile uses that loader | Ordinary download requests for the loader. |
| Modrinth | When you browse, install or update mods, packs or modpacks, open the Mods page, or export a profile | Your searches, and the SHA-1 hashes of the mod files in the profile so Modrinth can tell which mods and versions they are. |
| GitHub | Each time Auroria starts, unless you turn off **Settings, Launcher, Check for updates** | A request for the latest Auroria release. Nothing about you or your computer. |
| mclo.gs | Only when you press **Upload** on the logs page or the crash popup | The game log you chose to upload. It becomes a public link. |
| Minecraft servers | When you save a server or open the Servers tab | A status ping to that server's address, like the in-game server list. |
| Discord, on your computer | While a game runs, unless you turn off **Settings, Launcher, Show in Discord** | The profile name and Minecraft version, sent to the Discord app on your own computer, which shows it on your profile. |

## Changes Auroria makes to your system

- **Start menu:** when notifications are on, Auroria adds an "Auroria" entry to your Start menu. Windows only shows notifications for apps that have one. Turn off **Settings, Launcher, Show notifications** and delete the entry to remove it.
- **Desktop:** Auroria only adds a desktop shortcut when you choose "Desktop shortcut" on a profile.
- **Update files:** when you update in place, Auroria replaces its own exe and keeps nothing else.
